What people ask us first

What does a society playground cost?

It depends on how much equipment the space takes and what goes under it. A single multi-activity structure with a few solo items costs very differently from a full zoned playground with rubber surfacing. Send the area and a photo, and we will give you a built-up figure covering equipment, foundations, surfacing and installation.

How much space do I need?

More than the footprint of the equipment. Every structure needs a clear safety zone around it, and slides and swings need more on the exit side. The layout drawing marks the structures and clearances on your plan, so you can see what fits before anything is ordered.

Do I need safety flooring under the play area?

Under anything children can fall from, yes. We supply EPDM rubber flooring with the equipment, so the surface and structures are quoted and installed together.

Does outdoor gym equipment need a trainer?

No. The stations use body weight and the user's own resistance, with nothing to load or adjust. For a mixed-age society we usually put a multi-station unit at the centre with cardio and mobility items around it.

Will the equipment survive a Kolkata monsoon?

The play and gym structures use galvanised, powder-coated steel with rotomoulded and FRP components, all specified for outdoor use. Signage is moulded FRP with colour cast into the composite, so there is no painted surface to blister and no rivet joint for water to get into.

Can signage be made in our brand colour?

Yes. There are 26 standard colours across all ten pylon profiles, with bespoke matches against order. Faces take vinyl or acrylic embossed graphics.

Do you install, or only supply?

Both. Site assessment, supply, installation and finishing are handled as one job, so you have one point of contact for the finished space.
