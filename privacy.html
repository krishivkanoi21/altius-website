Privacy

This page explains what happens to your details when you use the Altius Realty Services website.

Enquiries

When you send the enquiry form, your details and the page you were on go by email to our office through Web3Forms, a form delivery service. We use them to reply to your enquiry.

If you tap a WhatsApp link, your message goes through WhatsApp under its own terms. Calls and emails reach us directly.

Advertising and cookies

We advertise on Google. This site uses a Google Ads tag and cookies to count when someone who came from one of our ads sends the form, opens WhatsApp or taps our phone number. It does not send the details you type into the form to Google.

You can manage how Google uses data for ads in your Google ad settings, or clear or block cookies in your browser.

Other services on this site

Pages load fonts from Google Fonts, and our contact section shows a Google Map. Google can receive your device's internet address when these load.
